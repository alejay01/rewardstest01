## The Boudin Company Rewards Privacy Policy

## Information We Collect

We may collect:

- Contact information, such as name, mobile number, and email address.
- Rewards information, such as points balance, visit history, coupon status, and redemption history.
- Signup information, such as QR code source, kiosk source, signup location, consent status, and policy version.
- Marketing preference information, such as SMS, email, and push opt-in or opt-out status.
- Technical information, such as IP address, browser, device type, user agent, and timestamps.
- Message information, such as messages sent, delivery status, HELP requests, STOP requests, and campaign activity.
- Optional profile information, such as birthday month/day and favorite location.

## How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Create and manage rewards accounts.
- Award points and issue coupons.
- Redeem coupons and prevent duplicate or fraudulent redemptions.
- Send account, reward, coupon, and program messages.
- Send marketing SMS, email, or push notifications when you have opted in.
- Respond to HELP requests, customer questions, and support requests.
- Record and honor opt-out requests.
- Improve the rewards program, offers, reporting, and customer experience.
- Maintain security, audit records, and legal/compliance records.

## SMS Messaging

If you opt in to SMS messages, The Boudin Company Rewards may send recurring automated marketing text messages with rewards, coupons, and special offers. Message frequency varies, up to 4 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

You may opt out of marketing texts at any time by replying `STOP`. You may request help by replying `HELP` or calling 713-561-5645.

We do not sell or share SMS opt-in data, SMS consent records, or mobile phone numbers with third parties for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

## Email And Push Notifications

If you opt in to email or push notifications, we may send rewards updates, coupons, program notices, and special offers. You may unsubscribe from marketing email through the unsubscribe link in the email. You may disable push notifications in your browser or device settings.

## How We Share Information

We may share information with service providers that help us operate the rewards program, such as:

- Website, hosting, database, and security providers.
- SMS providers, including Telnyx or a replacement provider if needed.
- Email and push notification providers.
- Analytics, reporting, and fraud-prevention tools.
- Professional advisors, legal counsel, or authorities when required by law or needed to protect rights, safety, or security.

We do not sell customer rewards data. We do not sell or share SMS opt-in data or mobile phone numbers with third parties for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

## Your Choices

You can:

- Reply `STOP` to opt out of marketing SMS.
- Reply `HELP` for SMS help.
- Use email unsubscribe links to stop marketing email.
- Disable push notifications in your browser or device settings.
- Contact us to request access, correction, deletion, or update of your rewards information, subject to legal, security, and operational limits.

## Data Retention

We keep rewards, coupon, consent, and message records for as long as needed to operate the program, honor opt-outs, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, comply with legal obligations, and maintain business records. Consent and opt-out records may be retained even after an account is deleted so we can prove and honor messaging preferences.

## Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Children

The rewards program is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated policy will be posted with a new effective date. Continued use of the rewards program after an update means the updated policy applies.
